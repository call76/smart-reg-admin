Why you can trust the advice

Transparent by design — no black boxes

Every recommendation shows its reasoning, so farmers and officers can check it instead of guessing.

  • Explained scores. Temperature, rainfall, soil pH, season and water are shown separately.
  • Approved fertilizer rates only. An extension officer signs off each rate and its source.
  • Honest data labels. Demo, live and stale data are always marked.
  • Forecasts with ranges. Experimental predictions show confidence, never false certainty.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who can use KIZA-AGRI?

Farmers, buyers and traders can register themselves. Government officers and extension officers receive accounts from an administrator.

Does it need a smartphone app?

No. It works in any modern phone or computer browser, with a simple mode for first-time users.

Where does the data come from?

Administrators load official data (for example RAB, MINAGRI, NISR) and live weather. Every source is recorded and any demo data is clearly labelled.

Can I trust the fertilizer advice?

Rates are only shown as approved after an extension officer confirms them with a source. Unapproved rates carry a visible warning.

Is my information safe?

Accounts use hashed passwords, role-based access and protected forms, and important actions are written to an audit log.
